## Provisional selection

C03 is selected because it has the clearest unequal branch without resembling two doors or a binary menu. The left route is open, brighter, deeper, and curved; the right route is narrower, nearer, soffit-defined, and partially occluded. The central wall prevents either route from becoming a centered hero path. Both route silhouettes support non-overlapping desktop and mobile polygons, and each offers specific continuity anchors for immediate follow-up generation.

The selection remains provisional. `public/scenes/scenes.json` isolates the image URL and path geometry so a later approved replacement does not require navigation logic changes.
